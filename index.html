Dil Se

kuch baatein jo dil mein thi

01

Main jaanta hoon…

Har baar main hi pehle sorry bol deta hoon. Kabhi-kabhi lagta hai ki yeh meri aadat ban gayi hai.

Is baar maine socha, sirf words nahi… thoda time aur effort bhi dena chahiye.

02

Block hone ke baad jo silence thi, woh sunai deti thi.

Main yeh nahi keh raha ki main perfect tha. Galatiyaan hui, misunderstanding hui, aur main unhe theek se handle nahi kar paya.

03

Main maafi maangta hoon.

Us cheez ke liye jiski wajah se tumhe distance lena pada.

Us feeling ke liye jo tumhe hui, chahe main us waqt samajh na paya hoon.

04

Main yeh nahi keh raha ki sab kuch turant theek ho jaye.

Main sirf yeh kehna chahta hoon ki main yahan hoon… jab tum ready ho.

Koi pressure nahi. Koi demand nahi.

Bas itna sa…

Agar kabhi mann kare baat karne ka, main sunne ke liye yahin hoon.
Aur agar abhi space chahiye, woh bhi bilkul theek hai.

— Prince
